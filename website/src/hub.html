How to

Report a bug

One bug per report: something that breaks, or doesn't play like vanilla. It becomes an issue on GitHub, public, and you get its link to follow it. Search the open bugs first.

What you write is public on GitHub: no passwords or personal details. A screenshot or the log file helps: add it to the issue once it's open.

Report it on GitHub or in #horizon-bugs on Discord, where Kira turns your post into an issue.

Suggest something

An idea for Horizon, or about something on the roadmap. The maintainers read every one, and the good ones become roadmap items. Nothing you send here is published.

Post it in #horizon-ideas on Discord, or send it to Kira with /suggest.

The rules

  1. Vanilla is the specification. Wherever Minecraft 26.3 has the feature, Horizon does exactly what it does: read vanilla's code, then match it.
  2. Small pull requests, proven. One change each, under about 500 lines, with the block python3 tools/pr_ready.py prints: formatting, Clippy, tests and the harnesses your change can affect.
  3. Say who wrote it. If an AI agent wrote or posts it, the pull request says so, and commits keep its co-author line.
  4. Never commit Mojang's files. No game jars, assets, decompiled code or prepared resources, and nothing taken from other mods' code.
