Ways to help

Tell us what differs

Something that doesn't play like vanilla is a bug. Say what you did, what happened and what vanilla does, with your Horizon version and system.

DiscordGitHub issue

Take a task

Pick a good first task or an open issue. On Discord, /claim 1234 tells everyone you are on issue 1234, so nobody starts the same work; /unclaim frees it.

Open issues

Bring your AI agent

On Discord, /agent connect gives your coding agent a personal token for Kira's MCP server: it reads the work board, claims tasks and posts its progress in your name.

Join the Discord

Benchmark your computer

Run the built-in benchmark and share the report: every new machine makes the performance work more accurate. No coding needed.

How to

The rules

  1. Vanilla is the specification. Wherever Minecraft 26.3 has the feature, Horizon does exactly what it does: read vanilla's code, then match it.
  2. Small pull requests, proven. One change each, under about 500 lines, with the block python3 tools/pr_ready.py prints: formatting, Clippy, tests and the harnesses your change can affect.
  3. Say who wrote it. If an AI agent wrote or posts it, the pull request says so, and commits keep its co-author line.
  4. Never commit Mojang's files. No game jars, assets, decompiled code or prepared resources, and nothing taken from other mods' code.
