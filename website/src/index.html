What it is

Horizon reimplements the Minecraft: Java Edition 26.3 client and its game logic in Rust, one subsystem at a time, and compares each piece with the vanilla game. Each of its {{HARNESSES}} differential harnesses drives the same inputs through Horizon and through the vanilla client and server, from your own copy of the game, and checks that the outputs are identical.

Every subsystem now matches vanilla in every harness that compares it. That is {{PERCENT}} of what the harnesses compare, not proof that nothing else differs: the remaining known differences are listed under What's next.

The native client runs on Linux, macOS on Apple Silicon and Windows. It opens on its own title screen, generates worlds with a Rust port of the vanilla generators and draws them with vanilla's block models, lighting, sky and fog.

Your own copy
Install Java Edition with the official launcher and play 26.3 once. Horizon builds its resources from those files on your machine and never downloads or shares game files.
For the setup
Python 3.10 or newer and Java 25 for the one-time resource preparation; a GPU with Vulkan on Linux and Windows, or any Apple Silicon Mac.
