# Horizon v0.2.97 vs vanilla 26.3 on Windows 11: Ryzen 7 9800X3D + RTX 5080 (2026-10-29)

The first `tools/perf_bench.py` run on Windows ([docs/benchmark.md](../../docs/benchmark.md)): Horizon, vanilla 26.3 and
vanilla with performance mods, render distances 8, 16 and 32, 1280x720, three games each, every game from a fresh
world. The reports are the `*-win-9800x3d-5080-rd*.json` files next to this page.

```sh
python tools/perf_bench.py --table horizon-win-9800x3d-5080 vanilla-win-9800x3d-5080 vanilla-mods-win-9800x3d-5080
```

## Setup

- **Machine:** AMD Ryzen 7 9800X3D (8 cores, 16 threads), 94 GB RAM (DDR5, EXPO off), NVIDIA GeForce RTX 5080
  (driver 617.42), Windows 11 Pro 26200, Balanced power plan, desktop. One 1920x1080 120 Hz display. The game windows
  were on the desktop of an unlocked session.
- **Horizon:** release v0.2.97 as the launcher installs it (`--build %APPDATA%\horizon-launcher\versions\v0.2.97`,
  `--resources` its `.local\horizon-client\26.3`), Vulkan.
- **Vanilla:** the 26.3 client jar with Fabric Loader 0.19.5 and the driver mod, Java 25.0.5 (Temurin), `-Xmx2G`
  with the launcher's G1 flags and the manifest's `-XX:StackShadowPages=32`, OpenGL 3.3.
- **Vanilla + mods:** the same with Sodium mc26.3-0.9.2, Lithium mc26.3-0.26.2, FerriteCore 9.0.0 and
  ImmediatelyFast 1.17.1 (`tools/benchmark-deps/mods.lock.json`).
- **Commands:** `python tools/perf_bench.py --game <game> --label <game>-win-9800x3d-5080 --distances <d>
  --sizes 1280x720 --repeats 3` (plus `--build` and `--resources` for Horizon), with the games taking turns at
  each distance.
- **Memory** is the working set on Windows (what Task Manager shows), sampled from outside the process. Horizon's
  GPU figure is its own Vulkan allocator total. `nvidia-smi` gives no per-process figure under WDDM, so vanilla has
  none.

## What it shows

- At RD 8 and 16, Horizon's average FPS is 18–41 % above vanilla's in every scene. Its 1% lows are 1.2–1.9 times
  vanilla's and its 0.1% lows 2.0–2.8 times. On this CPU and GPU the performance mods leave vanilla's averages
  level or slightly lower, and raise its 1% lows by 8–23 %.
- At RD 32, Horizon is ahead in the forest, mountain and village averages. Over the ocean its average is level with
  vanilla's, and its lows are about half of vanilla's and of the modded client's (113 vs 228 and 296 FPS 1% low).
  That scene is worth a closer look, though builds were running during Horizon's RD 32 games.
- Horizon reaches the title screen 2–2.4 times faster than vanilla (about 3 times faster than the modded client)
  and loads a scene's chunks 3–4 times faster. Its steady working set is 38–46 % smaller.
- Each game uses its own graphics API: Horizon Vulkan, vanilla OpenGL. That difference is part of what's compared.
